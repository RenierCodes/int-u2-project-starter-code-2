Healthy Mind Healthy Life


Mental health affects how we think, feel, and act in our daily lives. It influences how we handle stress, relate to others, and make decisions. Mental health is important for overall well-being. By learning about and increasing awareness of mental health, we can improve our quality of life and support those around us.

Did you know? Approximately 1 in 5 adults in the United States experiences mental illness each year. This highlights the importance of mental health awareness and the need for support and resources for those affected.

Myths Vs. Facts

Myth: People with mental illness are violent.

Facts: Most people with mental illness are not violent. In fact, they are more likely to be victims of violence than perpetrators.

Myth: Mental illness is a sign of weakness.

Facts: Mental illness is a medical condition, not a character flaw. It can affect anyone, regardless of their strength of character.
